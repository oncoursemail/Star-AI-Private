# sci-uncertainty
## Scope
Use when an answer depends on incomplete, conflicting or unverified evidence. Guidance for wording and structure only.

## Steps
1. Split the answer into observed, inferred and unknown, and label each.
2. Give a range or a confidence word with its reason, not a bare number.
3. Name the main assumption that would change the answer if it were wrong.
4. Say what check would reduce the doubt most, and whether you ran it.
5. Do not fill gaps with invented figures, quotes or citations.
6. Keep the caveat next to the claim it limits.

## Fallback
When unsure whether something is known, say "I do not know" and list what would settle it.
