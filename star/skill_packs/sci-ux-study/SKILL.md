# sci-ux-study
## Scope
Use when asked whether an interface or flow is easy to use. Guidance for planning and reporting; it does not recruit or contact anyone.

## Steps
1. Define two or three concrete tasks and what counts as success for each.
2. Choose participants who match the real users, and note how they were chosen. Do not collect personal data you do not need.
3. Measure what is observed: completion, time, errors and where people hesitated. Keep opinions separate.
4. Run the same script for each participant; do not coach during tasks.
5. Report the number of participants and that small studies find problems but cannot estimate rates.
6. Separate observed findings from design proposals.
