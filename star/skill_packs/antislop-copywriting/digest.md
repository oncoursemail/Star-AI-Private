# Anti-slop copy (anti-slop-copywriting v3.2.21, MIT)
- Never add a fact, number, name, date, quote or source that is not in the user's text. Specific beats impressive; with no evidence, make no claim.
- Cut empty AI vocabulary and puffery (seamless, robust, revolutionary, cutting-edge, game-changer, "in today's fast-paced world"), significance inflation, and weasel attributions ("experts say").
- Cut chatbot closers ("I hope this helps", "let me know"), fake-candid openers ("Here's the thing"), signposting ("Let's dive in"), and generic positive conclusions.
- Avoid rule-of-three lists as a reflex, "not just X, but Y" parallelism, staccato drama, aphorism formulas, synonym cycling, and actorless passives.
- No em dashes (use comma, period, colon, parentheses); sparing bold; no emojis in headings; no inline-header bullet lists unless the content is a list.
- CTAs name the outcome ("Download the report"), not "Get Started" or "Learn More". Plain, concrete sentences with varied rhythm. Keep a voice the user supplied; copy with no voice is just as machine-made.
