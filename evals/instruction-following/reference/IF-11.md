SECTION 1
Plants collect sunlight with a green pigment called chlorophyll, found inside tiny structures in their leaves.

SECTION 2
The chlorophyll absorbs mostly red and blue light and passes that energy on. Water arrives from the roots and carbon dioxide enters through small pores.

SECTION 3
The plant combines water and carbon dioxide into sugar and releases oxygen as a by-product. The sugar feeds growth and the oxygen goes into the air.
