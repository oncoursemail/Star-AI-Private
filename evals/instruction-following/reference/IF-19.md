Once upon a midnight, a small fox named Pip could not sleep. The moon was big and round, and it sat right above his den like a silver plate. Pip crept out into the cold grass and looked up. A owl on a branch blinked at him and asked what he wanted. Pip said he wanted to know why the moon followed him home each night. The owl smiled and said the moon is a good friend who likes to watch over every sleepy fox until the sun comes up.
