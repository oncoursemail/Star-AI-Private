A rainbow appears when sunlight shines through raindrops. Sunlight looks white but it is really a mix of every colour. When a ray of light enters a raindrop it slows down and bends a little. Each colour bends by a different amount so the colours start to spread apart. Inside the drop the light bounces off the back wall and heads out again. As it leaves the drop it bends once more and spreads out even further. Your eyes catch the separated colours coming from thousands of drops at once. You need the sun behind you and rain in front of you to see one. Isn't science wonderful?
