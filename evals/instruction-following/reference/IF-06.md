Mira was asked to count the books on a very tall shelf, so she counted one and asked her friend to count the rest. Her friend did the same, and so on down the line.

***

Each friend kept a note on a stack of paper that said how many books they had seen so far. The last friend reached the empty shelf, the base case, and answered zero.

***

Then the answers came back up the line, each friend adding one. Mira added her own book last and announced the total. That is recursion: a smaller problem, a stopping point and a stack of waiting answers.
