STEP 1
Fill a kettle with fresh cold water and bring it to a boil. Warm the teapot with a splash of hot water and pour it out.

STEP 2
Add one **teaspoon of loose leaves** for each cup, plus one extra for the pot.

STEP 3
Pour in the boiling water and let it steep for **three to five minutes**, depending on how strong you like it.

STEP 4
Strain the tea into cups and add milk or lemon if you like. Pour the rest into a thermos to keep it warm.
