Games

Three projects, one launcher.

Each project below lists its roadmap items by state. The lists come straight from the roadmap behind the progress page, so they cannot drift apart. The pictures are concept art, not screenshots.

Metallic RC

A Rec Room revival

Metallic RC is our own revival of the shutdown-era Rec Room client, with a backend we run ourselves and creator tools on top. It is not a finished game. The big open question is whether two people can share a room on the real client, and that is the next goal.

Metallic MC

Minecraft, on your terms

Metallic MC is about running Minecraft servers: on your own hardware first, hosted by us later. The management tools exist and are tested in pieces. Launching the game with your own account and real hosting are still ahead. You will always need your own Minecraft account.

Metallic RB

Roblox experiences

Metallic RB collects every Metallic project on Roblox. It starts with Paintball. Metallic opens these in the Roblox app; you keep your Roblox account and Roblox keeps running the game.

PX is not a game.

PX is how we show our work. It is the progress page.
