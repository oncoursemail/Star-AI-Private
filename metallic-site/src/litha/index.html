Litha

An architect for your game ideas.

Litha lives in the Metallic launcher and answers questions about building games: how to script a door in Roblox, why a Unity object falls through the floor, how a Minecraft datapack is laid out.

  • Short answers. A few sentences, or one small piece of code when code helps.
  • Friendly for all ages. She keeps replies kind, and tells people not to share passwords or personal details.
  • Honest. If she is not sure, she says so instead of guessing.
  • Optional. One switch in settings turns her off. While she is off, the add-on makes no network requests at all.

What she is and is not

Litha is a helper, not a person.

She is an AI that answers through an engine Metallic runs itself. She is separate from STAR, our other project, and she cannot see your files or your account. Answers can be wrong, so test what she suggests.
