Changelog

What's new in Metallic

Every launcher release from 0.2.7 on, newest first. Metallic updates itself.

Metallic 0.2.17

November 6, 2026
  • Linux: Metallic no longer freezes when its window comes back to the front, for example after a revival's installer closes.

Metallic 0.2.16

November 6, 2026
  • New: Metallic for Android phones and tablets. It shows live counts, news and links. It does not install revivals on a phone.
  • New: Metallic for Mac, Apple silicon and Intel. It isn't signed by Apple yet, so macOS asks you to allow it the first time. Revivals are Windows games and none has been seen running on a Mac yet.
  • Metallic for Quest is updated: no black screen while it starts, a lighter wallpaper, and your own backgrounds.
  • Linux: if the window freezes or stays black, Metallic recovers by itself and uses safe graphics next time.
  • New in Settings: Start Steam with a revival. Play opens Steam in the background with no window. It's off until you turn it on.
  • One slow moment no longer turns your own background into a still picture.
  • Metallic takes less room: it removes what older builds left behind and limits its own cache. Your settings, backgrounds and revivals stay.

Metallic 0.2.15

November 5, 2026
  • Add your own background in Settings: a picture, a GIF or a video (MP4 or WebM). It has no sound and rests while a game runs. On the Linux AppImage, use WebM.
  • Play on NodeNet goes straight into the game on Windows. Turn on Open their launcher if you want their launcher instead.
  • The X beside Running asks the game to close first, and tells you if it is still running.
  • Your Discord status shows the revival's picture, with Metallic small in the corner.
  • New tags for revivals: Maintenance, Beta, Early access and Closed beta.
  • The Running row is tidier.
  • Recora has its website and a live player count.
  • The Playground background is gone.

Metallic 0.2.14

November 5, 2026
  • Metallic opens in about half the time on our test PC. On Windows a small crow shows straight away while it starts, and the loading screen is about 3 seconds. Click it to skip.
  • New in Settings, under Starting up: Skip the intro, and Lazy loading, which loads the background and sounds after the page. Both are off until you turn them on.
  • An X beside Running closes the game straight away. Unsaved progress is lost.
  • The big revival panel is cleaner, and each revival shows a Released or Not released tag.
  • Storage has a Move all button that puts your revivals in the Metallic folder.
  • Sort revivals by Discord members and filter by PC, VR or Quest. The dropdowns are dark now.
  • If you excluded Metallic from Windows Defender, a new folder in a later version gets excluded too. Windows asks you once.
  • A revival no longer shows Down after one missed check.

Metallic 0.2.13

November 4, 2026
  • A new loading screen: every revival's logo drifts in around the Metallic crow, they come together into one, and a shine turns it silver. It plays each time Metallic starts and takes about 5 seconds.
  • Slower PCs, Linux without a GPU and reduced motion get a still crow instead.

Metallic 0.2.12

November 4, 2026
  • Metallic's own soundtrack, four new tracks. Music is now off by default, though if you've saved settings before, yours may stay as it was.
  • First snow, a half Thanksgiving, half Christmas background, on by default from November 20 to December 26. You can pick it any time in Settings.
  • The Revival wall is the new default background.
  • Metallic starts lighter: pictures load when you scroll near them.
  • Your Discord status comes back by itself after Discord restarts.
  • A debug log in Settings, off by default, so you can send us what went wrong. It stays on your device.
  • Revival news refreshes by itself from 19 sources, with real dates.
  • 9 more revivals listed: Rec Room Legacy, RestoRoom, BlueRoom, Rec Room Patches, RecUnion, RecVerse, RecWorks, Recora and PrismNet. They have Discord numbers only, and we haven't confirmed their games work.
  • A Version filter on the revivals page, by Rec Room year.
  • Every revival card shows its description, and revival panels have rebuilt timelines.
  • NodeNet can now be installed from Metallic on Windows and Linux.
  • Solar Lab has a Download button and a live player count. The download opens a Gofile page, and nobody has checked that file.
  • A fix for having to press Play twice in MeowNet. It hasn't been tested on the real MeowNet launcher yet, so tell us if it still happens.
  • Linux players on 0.2.10 or older: the update bar won't appear, so use Settings > Check for updates or download again.

Metallic 0.2.11

November 4, 2026
  • Linux: fixes a startup fault and slow drawing that could make Metallic freeze.
  • Linux: a window that stops answering now recovers by itself in a graphics safe mode, which Settings can turn off.

Metallic 0.2.10

November 4, 2026
  • Linux: pressing Install or any other button no longer crashes Metallic, and it no longer needs a reboot to open again.
  • Linux: sounds work.
