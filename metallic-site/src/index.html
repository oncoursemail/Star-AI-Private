    Installing Metallic
    InstallDownloads this revival's client
    PlayPCVR
    PlayUp to date. Updated by itself.

    1. To begin, download the installer and run it. One window holds every revival, its news and its live numbers.

    2. Then pick a revival and press Install. Metallic fetches its client and keeps it beside the others.

    3. Press Play and choose PC or VR: desktop mode on your monitor, or your headset.

    4. From then on, updates install by themselves. Each new build is signed and checked first.

    Play it on PC.

    The same switch sits under every Play button in Metallic. PC for keyboard and mouse, VR for your headset. Try it.
