    How it works

    From download to in game in three steps.

    1. 1

      Install Metallic

      Download the installer and run it. One window holds every revival, its news and its live numbers.

    2. 2

      Pick a revival

      Press Install. Metallic fetches that revival's client and keeps it beside the others.

    3. 3

      Play on PC or in VR

      Choose desktop mode or your headset under the Play button. Updates install by themselves, signed and checked first.
