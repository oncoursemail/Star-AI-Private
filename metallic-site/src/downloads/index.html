What you need

Windows 10 or 11

64-bit (x86-64). Metallic uses the WebView2 runtime that ships with Windows; the installer adds it if it is missing.

An internet connection

For the live counts and the news, and to install and update revivals.

A headset, for VR

Optional. Revivals that support VR start in your headset when you pick VR under the Play button.

Updates install by themselves

You download Metallic once. When a new build is out, the launcher fetches it, checks its signature and installs it. There is nothing to download again from this page.
