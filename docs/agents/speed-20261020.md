decoding is lossless by construction (each accepted draft token is the target model's own greedy choice under that

Recommend `parallel = 3` + `ctx-size = 98304` for [star-fast] (cost ~0.9 GB RSS), BUT only together with the
client-side rule the proposal already states (night school, evals and agents send at most two star-fast requests at
a time): then a Discord/desktop message during night school answers in ~2 s instead of ~33 s, at the price of the
two background streams running ~15% slower and the chat itself at ~4 tok/s while they run. Without that rule the
third slot just moves the queue (53 s with three busy). Not switched here (measure only); before switching, repeat
the 3-slot / 1-background sample (3.7 s first token looked worse than 2 slots).
