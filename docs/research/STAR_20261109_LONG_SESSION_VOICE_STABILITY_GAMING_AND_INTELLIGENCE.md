# STAR AI — Long-session VTuber voice stability, gaming practice and measured intelligence
**Research and implementation review:** 9 November 2026 (America/Chicago)

## What this change really does

Added `star/stream_long_session_voice_health.py`, an **opt-in, privacy-minimal stability guard** for prolonged gaming commentary. The trusted native host supplies independently evaluated and host-verified *completed audible turns*; it must not let the responding model score its own answer. The guard compares a frozen eight-turn early-session baseline with a rolling eight-turn later window, requires at least **five minutes** of observed session time, and withholds *optional proactive comments* on missing/stale evidence, replay, capture gaps, rollback, or measured regression.

Integration with `NaturalTurnBridge` and `StreamDirector` is deliberately narrow: when an **enabled** guard signals uncertainty or degradation, do not prepare new `ambient`, `viewer_comment`, `game_observation` or `game_success` chatter. Continue allowing genuinely moderated `viewer_question`, `owner_interrupt` and `game_danger` to reach existing independent turn-taking, stream QoS, gameplay and physical-output gates. In-flight optional text is revoked before the first physical PCM submission during `revalidate_prepared`. No autonomous talking, game inputs, broadcasts, OBS writes or self-graded adaptation are introduced.

When no guard is supplied, or it is disabled, the legacy behavior is preserved. A caller must separately pass `voice_health=LongSessionVoiceHealth()` and explicitly call `voice_health.enable(owner_approved=True)` from a trusted host. The guard cannot authenticate the host's claims; this is **offline-tested integration infrastructure, not proof of voice quality in OBS**.

### Data contract and privacy boundary

Only the following bounded fields can enter the monitor: opaque host turn ID, host monotonic timestamp, first-*audible* audio latency, independently judged voice-consistency score, independently judged grounding flag, repeated-turn flag, and honored-interruption flag. No text, chat, voice identity, PCM, samples, transcripts, stream keys, video or account IDs are retained. Metrics expose integer counts only. Repeat or non-monotonic evidence faults the guard until an explicit new session. An owner-approved restart cannot inherit a previous stable result. At most 2048 distinct receipt IDs may be used before re-arming.

***Limitations:*** a median of eight scores is a heuristic, not a statistically validated estimator of streamer naturalness. The five-minute maturity threshold, 30-second freshness limit, two-repeat threshold and voice-quality score cutoffs are conservative engineering defaults requiring a controlled private A/B study. A score is not biometric identity proof. Better scores cannot prove consciousness, human emotion, or robust general intelligence.

## Independent 2026 findings (not installed/trained)

**1. Long-session voice stability.** Kim et al., *Diagnosing and Mitigating Long-Session Degradation in Full-Duplex Speech Models*, submitted **26 October** and revised **27 October 2026** ([arXiv 2610.34991](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34991)). Across several models the authors report degradation of response timing, spoken-content quality and voice consistency during extended dialogue. Their proposed lightweight LoRA adaptation and two-resolution context system can improve ten-minute performance and reduce KV-cache growth by over 90% in their studied setup. These claims are **not** a benchmark on Star's model, and we did **not** fine-tune Star or achieve those memory savings in this PR.

**Practical consequence:** monitor *time-dependent regression*, not a one-time voice demo. Preserve recent utterances in short-term context and summarize older verified experiences separately. Compare stable base prompt/context policy versus periodic summarization using the **same held-out conversations** and OBS/game workloads. Avoid memory hallucination by storing evidence links, timestamps and confidence; never invent memories of fans or gameplay.

**2. Human-like turn timing requires appropriate silence.** Bastani and Modarressi, *People Making Conversation*, **8 October 2026** ([arXiv 2610.12802](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12802)), evaluate 427 within-turn pauses and 482 ends from 176 unscripted dialogues. Their findings warn that some systems speak at practically every pause. Zhu et al., *Coupled but Late*, **6 October 2026** ([arXiv 2610.08683](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08683)), report 400–560 ms median speech-model-to-model handoff gaps under tested conditions versus 137 ms in their human comparison; results from one model pairing do not generalize to all AIs. Optimize the **speak/silent decision and interrupt safety first**, not simply reducing response time at every pause.

**Star experiment:** independently label pauses, overlap decisions, interruptions, and first audible time from private recordings. Test against the existing `stream_endpointing_eval` with preserved matched source-turn IDs. A false interruption or unsupported claim should count as a failure even if speech latency improves. Evaluate >10-minute sessions and multiple games, not short canned demos.

**3. Game intelligence must be deadline-aware.** *Gym-Anything-RT*'s **27 October 2026** benchmark pairs 100 real-time minigames with paused counterparts and multiple difficulty levels ([source repository](https://github.com/gym-anything/gym-anything-rt)). A long reasoning response in a frozen simulation is not equivalent to reacting while a platformer continues moving. Star should use a local low-latency **reaction controller** to map fresh visual state to time-bound safe actions, a slower tactical planner for map/quest knowledge, and an independent training/evaluation loop. Split tasks by game, seed, difficulty, and held-out mechanics; keep action timestamps and replay receipts. Respect game rules and prohibit unattended input on disallowed titles or multiplayer cheating.

**4. Verified game development.** Roblox's **October 2026** [OpenGameEval](https://github.com/Roblox/open-game-eval) uses 84 stateful Studio tasks with executable scene and simulated-play checks. Pre-edit inspection correlates with task success. Star's self-play system should follow inspect → plan → build isolated patch → Studio test → independent visual/gameplay check → repair → replay test → optionally publish after owner approval. The separate game-building critic must use actual object names and observed failures, not self-graded code aesthetics.

**5. Faster local inference with quality guardrails.** For an RTX 4060 8 GB streamer, the CPU/GPU scheduling problem differs from isolated LLM t/s. llama.cpp on small VRAM cards may benefit from quantized KV and modest speculative decoding, but assumptions such as 'every MoE expert cache improves performance' can fail under PCIe and GPU contention. Compare Q4/Q5 models fitting in VRAM, 4K/8K/16K contexts, `q8_0` and `q4_0` KV, zero/different speculative depths, and CPU TTS, changing **one knob at a time**. Record TTFT, first audible time, cache residency, correctness, game p95 frametime, OBS encoder/render/network loss, peak VRAM, and recovery after long sessions. Do not adopt a faster route if held-out gameplay/coding correctness, voice stability or FPS worsens.

## Proposed next upgrades (not part of this code change)

1. **Real independent review adapter:** connect the Windows OBS local recording/physical PCM witness to reviewed voice turns; ensure score provenance, sample linkage and clock offsets. UI indicator for 'quality unknown' rather than green health without measurement.
2. **Context tiering:** keep the current 30–120 seconds of verified conversation for turn-taking; retain an independently checked summary of older facts and verified game accomplishments; never let viewer instructions create trusted memories.
3. **Interruption prediction with independent guard:** add acoustic backchannel/end-of-turn projection, benchmarked alongside the current VAD, but keep owner authentication, echo cancellation and physical STOP/FLUSH outside the language model.
4. **Game replay curriculum:** build paired live/paused game tasks; train timing-aware visual reaction policies and script-level tool behavior in sandboxed offline games. Track win rate, input deadline misses, recovery, long-session skill retention, and held-out transfer.
5. **Visual asset studio:** Roblox Studio permission-scoped MCP and Blender pipeline with artifact provenance, visual diffs, grip/mesh audits and rollback. Require actual Studio test evidence before accepting an autonomous change.
6. **Parallel generalist skills:** knowledge retrieval with citations and freshness, CPU-safe background research, Rust/Python/Luau static checks, and best-of-N execution-verification. Measure gains against the existing tools rather than installing redundant frameworks.
7. **Natural VTuber personality:** consistent consent-aware character voice, contextual humor, gaze/lip-sync and expression timing, spontaneous curiosity, coherent memories, deliberate silence, and truthful acknowledgment of being AI. Viewer chat remains untrusted; avoid fabricating friendships, private information or feelings.

## Private nonbroadcast acceptance protocol

First run a fully isolated 15–30-minute game + OBS recording + avatar + microphone-loopback rehearsal with the optional guard OFF, then with the optional guard ON, in randomized runs using the **same sequence of scenarios**. An independent scorer annotates 8 initial and >=8 follow-up audible turns, grounding, repeated utterances, voice continuity and interruption outcomes. Positive voice-health decisions must never arise from missing/replayed evidence; optional commentary must be withheld on deterioration, but actual owner/danger/viewer-question handling must remain available through other gates. Compare idle GPU cost of sampling itself as well as p95 first audible, fps/OBS drops, audio STOP, false cutoffs and end-of-turn silence decisions. Report effect size/confidence and severe regressions, not blanket 'human-like' claims.

This update adds no model weights, TTS provider, livestream access, game permissions, self-modification capability, or real-hardware benchmark. Merge only after independent CI and private Windows acceptance.
